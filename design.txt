3.

The designs must completely break away from the traditional,
boring, purely corporate event style.
The visuals need to say: "This is not a regular seminar; this is a
-high octane tech movement."

When a viewer looks at a flyer, a billboard, or a social media post,
the instant subconscious message should be:

Premium & Elite: Edo State is now a tech adopting state, and the visuals must
look expensive, sleek, and highly polished to match that standard.

Hyper-Modern but Culturally Rooted: It shouldn't look like a copy & paste
tech template. The graphics must carry a subtle weight of
authority and pride that feels uniquely native to Benin City's legacy of elite
craftsmanship, translated into modern digital architecture.

4. The Feeling of "Convergence & Scale"
Because Benin Tech Fest has merged with Edo Startup Week
and expanded to 4 days, the visuals must feel massive and interconnected.

The Design Signal: Use layout structures where different design
elements (like sharp geometric grids, glowing network lines,
or typography) seamlessly lock together or overlap.

The Message Passed:"The entire ecosystem is uniting under one
roof. If you are not here, you are missing out on the biggest tech link-up of the year."

The designs should never feel static, flat, or quiet.
They need to feel alive, active, and moving forward.

DN: Respect the color hierarchy, reservethe vibrant brand colors strictly for
focal points and key information.

Prioritize readability over aesthetics by ensuring all dates, speaker titles, and
schedules are presented in clean, high-contrast, perfectly legible text.
